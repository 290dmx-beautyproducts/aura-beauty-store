Delivery policy

  1. Dispatch. Orders paid before 2:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, leave Nairobi the same day. Later orders leave the next morning.
  2. Delivery time. Expect your parcel in Mombasa within 24 to 48 hours of payment confirmation. Public holidays and heavy rain can add 24 hours.
  3. Updates. We message you when payment is received, when your parcel is dispatched, and when it reaches Mombasa.
  4. Unavailable items. If an item sells out, we offer a swap or a full M-Pesa refund within 2 hours.
  5. Receiving your order. Keep your phone on. After two missed calls the parcel returns to the depot and re-delivery fees apply.

Returns policy

  1. Hygiene first. Wigs and hair are returnable only if unworn, lace uncut, hair uncombed, unwashed and unstyled, free of perfume, makeup and adhesive, with original tags and packaging.
  2. Time limit. Report within 24 hours of delivery. Return within 48 hours.
  3. Skincare. Sealed items only. Opened skincare is non-returnable for health safety. Damaged, leaking, expired or wrong items are replaced if reported within 24 hours with a photo or unboxing video.
  4. Refunds. After inspection we refund by M-Pesa within 24 hours. You cover return transport unless the error is ours.
