Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery, payment and returns.

How do I order?+
Add items to your cart, choose your delivery location, enter your name and area, then tap Checkout on WhatsApp. We receive your order, confirm stock and send payment details.
How long does delivery take?+
Orders paid before 2:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, are dispatched the same day. Expect your parcel within 24 to 48 hours of payment confirmation.
How much is delivery?+
Local Base is free over KSh 1,500, otherwise KSh 150. Mombasa Suburbs is KSh 250 and Upcountry is KSh 350.
How do I pay?+
By M-Pesa, after we confirm your items are in stock. We never ask for your M-Pesa PIN.
Can I return a wig?+
Yes, for hygiene reasons only if it is unworn, the lace is uncut, the hair is uncombed and unwashed, and it has its original tags and packaging. Report within 24 hours of delivery and return within 48 hours.
Can I return skincare?+
Sealed products only. Opened skincare cannot be returned for health reasons. If an item arrives damaged, leaking, expired or wrong, report it within 24 hours with a photo or unboxing video and we will make it right.
Are your products genuine and safe?+
We stock sealed products with visible batch numbers and expiry dates, and we do not sell bleaching creams containing mercury or high-strength hydroquinone. Always patch-test, and see a dermatologist or pharmacist for persistent skin concerns.
Do you deliver outside Mombasa?+
Yes. Choose Upcountry at checkout and we send your parcel by bus courier to your nearest depot.
How do I post a reel?+
Log in or sign up, open Aura Reels, tap the + button, choose your video and add a caption. Please follow our Community Guidelines.
Is my information safe?+
We only collect what we need to deliver your order and run your account, and we never sell it. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.
