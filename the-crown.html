Wig care basics

  • Detangle gently from the ends upward.

  • Use products made for your wig type.

Hygiene returns policy

For hygiene and health reasons, we accept wig returns only if the item is unworn, lace uncut, hair uncombed, unwashed and unstyled, with original tags and packaging. Report within 24 hours of delivery and return within 48 hours.
