1. Ordering and prices

All prices are in Kenyan shillings (KSh). We confirm that an item is in stock on WhatsApp before you pay. If something is unavailable, we will offer a swap or refund you in full by M-Pesa within 2 hours. We may decline or cancel an order and refund you, for example if there is a pricing error.

2. Payment

We accept payment by M-Pesa. We dispatch your order once your payment is confirmed in our M-Pesa records.

3. Delivery

Orders paid before 2:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, are dispatched the same day. Expect delivery within 24 to 48 hours of payment confirmation. Public holidays, weather and transport delays can add time. Delivery fees: Local Base is free over KSh 1,500, otherwise KSh 150; Mombasa Suburbs KSh 250; Upcountry KSh 350. Please keep your phone on, because our rider calls before arrival.

4. Returns and refunds

  • Wigs and hair: returnable only if unworn, lace uncut, hair uncombed, unwashed and unstyled, free of perfume, makeup and adhesive, with original tags and packaging. Report within 24 hours of delivery and return within 48 hours.
  • Skincare: sealed items only. Opened skincare cannot be returned, for health and safety reasons.
  • Damaged, leaking, expired or wrong items: report within 24 hours with a photo or unboxing video and we will replace or refund them.
  • Approved refunds are paid by M-Pesa within 24 hours of inspection. You pay return transport unless the mistake is ours.
