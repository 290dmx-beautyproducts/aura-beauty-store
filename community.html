Community Guidelines

Keep Aura Reels beautiful, safe and welcoming for everyone.

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · Tap a section to open it

1Be kind and respectful+

Treat everyone with respect. No bullying, harassment, insults, hate or discrimination based on skin tone, race, tribe, religion, gender, body type, disability or anything else.

2Keep it safe and appropriate+
  • No nudity, sexual content, graphic violence or dangerous activity.
  • No content that puts children at risk. Never post images of minors without a parent or guardian's permission.
3Share honest beauty content+
  • Do not promise guaranteed results, cures or "overnight" transformations.
  • Do not promote skin-lightening products that contain mercury, steroids or other harmful ingredients.
  • Be truthful about results. Show real before and after photos, not edited or borrowed ones.
  • Say clearly when a post is a paid promotion or you received the product free.
4No spam, scams or fake activity+

Do not post spam, misleading links, fake giveaways, fake reviews or repeated copies of the same video. Do not buy or trade likes and follows.

5Respect other people's work+

Only post videos, music and images that you created or have permission to use. Give credit where it is due.

6Protect privacy+

Do not film or share other people without their consent. Never post phone numbers, home addresses or other private details, yours or anyone else's.

7Reporting and enforcement+

See something that breaks these rules? Tap the three dots on a reel and choose Report. We review reports and may remove content, limit features or suspend accounts, depending on how serious the problem is. If you think we made a mistake, contact us through the Contact page and we will take another look.
