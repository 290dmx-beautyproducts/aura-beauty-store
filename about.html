Who We Are

Beauty, brought to your door

Aura Beauty Products began with a simple idea: every woman deserves skincare that works and hair that makes her feel like royalty, without having to travel to the city to find it. We bring carefully chosen skincare and wigs to your door in Mombasa and across Kenya, with easy ordering on WhatsApp, payment by M-Pesa and delivery in 24 to 48 hours.

Our Mission

To make safe, effective skincare and beautiful hair easy and affordable for every woman in Kenya, delivered with honesty and care.

Our Vision
